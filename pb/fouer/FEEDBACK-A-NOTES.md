## 1. "Make sprinting possible in walk mode, otherwise it's really slow"
- **What was wrong**: 2.0 m/s walking, 4.0 m/s running, and Shift was only read as `e.code === "ShiftLeft"` on keydown / keyup: a missed keyup (Cmd-Tab, a context menu) left the walker running or never ran at all in some Safari cases; there was no on-screen way to sprint, and the stick's sprint zone was the last 7 % of its travel (4 px).
- **Now** (`walk.js`): `WALK.speed` 2.6 m/s, `WALK.run` 6.5 m/s, `accel` 8 / `brake` 12 (exponential ease: ~0.35 s to full speed, ~0.25 s to a stop, at most ~0.9 m/s change per 60 Hz frame). Shift is read three ways (`e.key === "Shift"`, `e.code`, and `e.shiftKey` of **every** key / pointer event, so a lost keyup heals itself); window `blur` and tab hiding clear all keys and Shift. A **🏃 toggle** (`.wk-sprint`, 62 px, bottom right above the round button, touch and mouse) sprints until tapped again. A full stick push sprints on iPad: the pace blends in over 82 .. 97 % of the stick travel (`stickPace`, no jump), a light push still walks (0.35 m/s .. walking pace). The field of view widens by 5 degrees while sprinting. Pure helpers (`stickPace`, `paceFor`, `easeSpeed`) are harness-tested.
- Hint texts mention Shift / 🏃 / "push to the edge" (lb / de / en, `walk.hintKeys`, `walk.hintTouch`, `walk.sprint`).

## 2. "Like the Google Maps pin that you can drag"
- A **🧍 pin button** (`#pinBtn` in `pb/fouer.html`, under 👣, same visibility rules) in the builder view. Drag it onto the map: a ghost 🧍 follows (with a finger it hangs 58 px above it, so the tip, not the thumb, is the drop point; a mouse points with the tip), `renderer.screenToGround` gives the ground point, `walk.pinSpot` -> `pinLanding(mask, u, w)` snaps it to the nearest walkable open point within 10 m (a sealed pocket such as the hollow inside a closed hut is refused: `mask.openArea` flood fill), and `scene.setPin` draws the **ground marker** (ring + beam + facing arrow, green; red where nothing within 10 m can be walked; an amber ring + line at the raw drop point when it was snapped; it grows with the camera distance, so it stays visible from the overview). Letting go starts the walk there, facing the nearest built attraction within 60 m (else along the Haaptallee); letting go on the button again cancels; a plain tap only explains the button (toast); a plain tap on 👣 still starts at the last built attraction as before.
- Files: `ui.js` (gesture, ghost, `beginWalk`), `ui.css` (`#pinBtn`, `.pin-ghost`), `scene.js` (`setPin`, `pinState`), `walk.js` (`pinSpot`, `pinLanding`, `facingFrom`, `start(spot, opts)`), `main.js` (`hooks.pinPreview / setPin / startWalk(spot)`).

## 3. Loading screen that feels alive
- The loader is **inline in `pb/fouer.html`** (it must work before `main.js` and even when it never arrives). `window.__fouerLoad.set(p, label, ceiling, ms)` takes REAL progress: the 59 ES modules + three.js counted from Resource Timing while the code loads (0 .. 11 %), then `main.js` reports the world build (`createWorld` onProgress, 12 .. 62 %), the big models, the fair layer ("Stänn ginn opgestallt"), the lights, the cloud save, one tick per pitch model built (`scene.onApplied`, 95 .. 99 %). Between two real steps the bar **creeps** towards the step's ceiling with a Web Animation (`transform: scaleX`, compositor driven, so it keeps moving while the main thread builds models; every creep lasts at least 14 s, longer than the 12 s stall timer; ease-out, never reaches the ceiling by itself, never goes backwards) plus a moving shine. Step labels (lb / de / en): code, Glacis, rides, stalls, lights, crowd.
- Only if **nothing real** happened for 12 s: "Dauert méi laang wéi gewinnt …" and a retry button (reload). Real progress hides it again. `fail()` shows it at once (three.js missing, world failed).
- Flow `fouer-game` (step A1): holds the Glacis bake back by 2.2 s and samples the bar every 60 ms: monotonic, never static for more than 1 s (long main-thread tasks are skipped: the compositor keeps the bar moving but a sample cannot read it), the labels name the steps, no hint on a slow boot; a second page whose three.js never answers shows no hint at 9 s (bar still creeping) and the hint + retry at 14 s. Harness: the inline script runs against stubs (`harness-solids.js`).

## 4. + 5. "An invisible wall" at attractions and food spots; "an alley where you can't walk through"
- **What was wrong**: the pitch layer made every built or locked pitch ONE solid box = the whole slot (+0.2 m): 16 x 16 m for a Fouerfësch (the seating terrace, the forecourt and its tables were inside it), 30 x 18 m for a tent, 56 x 30 m for a coaster, 8 x 8 m for a chip stall. Ride bodies, tents with open fronts, terraces, queue areas and every gap between a ride and its neighbour that was narrower than the rectangle were walls you could see through. The NPC stalls were their grid footprints. In the old mask **215 000 cells (0.25 m) of open-looking ground (>= 1.2 m from any drawn solid) were blocked** on a full fair; now 200 (rim slivers).
- **Now** (`solids.js`, no table to go stale): what a body would hit is read from what is drawn. Every mesh of a pitch model (and of the merged base layer: NPC stalls, props, clutter) is rasterised on the ground at 0.1 m, counting only surfaces between **0.8 m (hip) and 1.75 m (head)**: walls, counters, ride frames, rails, table tops, lamp posts are solid; platforms, benches, floor plates, kerbs (< 0.8 m) are stepped over; awnings, signs, roofs, bunting, coaster track (> 1.75 m) are walked under. Cells merge into boxes (0.2 m cells: a box is at most 0.2 m bigger than its geometry, the rule is +-0.3 m). Consequences: tents with an open front are walk-in (the Bierzelt: walker reaches its middle), the Fouerfësch seating area is open between the tables, a stall counter can be approached to 0.5 m, the walkable station platform of a ride is open, a locked / NPC stall is solid exactly where its box is, an open plot is solid where its barrier and dressing are. Closed huts are hollow (only their walls), so a drop into one is refused by the pin (sealed pocket test).
  - Moving parts are not solid (`part:*`, and the big kit's `<id>-cars|rotor|gondolas|crown|seats|arm|ring|pod|cables|stays|boats-near`, the `far` LOD copy; a frozen pose would be an invisible wall where a bumper car once was). The `visible` flag is ignored (the fair layer hides the static children of a live ride and flips it with the camera). Transparent (< 0.7) and additive meshes are skipped.
  - Cost: 37 ms for the base layer + 32 ms for all 34 pitches on the Mac (headless Chromium), cached per model object, rebuilt when `scene.onApplied` says a model changed. `walk.js` got a bucket index (`makeBoxIndex`) for the thousands of boxes and `move()` is cut into pieces of at most 0.12 m so a thin wall is never skipped by a sprint on a slow frame. `WALK.radius` 0.34 -> 0.3.
  - `onFrontSide` now also accepts the front 70 % of the pitch rectangle itself (the walker stands in the seating area / at the counter and still gets "Iessen" / "Fueren").
- **The check** (`solids.js maskVsVisible`, `blockedOpenGaps`, flow `fouer-walk`, harness `harness-solids.js`): every drawn mesh of the scene (base batch + all 34 pit models) is rasterised independently at 0.1 m and compared with the mask on a 0.25 m grid: **blocked cells without a visible solid within 0.3 m** (invisible walls: 0 on the full fair, 0 on a mid-game fair with open plots and locked stalls) and **cells with >= 0.6 m clearance to every drawn solid (= a gap of >= 1.2 m) that the mask blocks** (200 cells, none bigger than a 17-cell sliver at the NE rim). The reverse (a visible solid the mask lets through): 99 of 67 000 cells (thin rails, poles). The harness proves the checks catch an invisible wall (a phantom box), a hole in a wall, and the old whole-rectangle collision (> 1000 invisible cells), and that a 1.2 / 1.5 / 2.5 m gap between two walls is passable and a 0.5 m slit is not.
- **Open question for part B / the model owner**: at level 3 and gold every stall gets `dressForecourt`: a continuous crowd barrier 0.95 m high, 7 m wide (13 m at the Fouerfësch) in front of the counter, with no gate. It is visible, so it is solid; the seating terrace of a gold Fouerfësch is entered through the 1.5 m gap between the rail's end and the pitch edge (the flow walks it). A 1.4 m gate in `dressForecourt` would make the counters of all level 3 stalls reachable from the front.
- Screenshot 3's alley: the screenshots do not say which pitch the Bierhaus stood on, so the alley was not pinned to one place; what the old mask got wrong there (a tent, rides and stalls blocking their whole slot, so every passage between them was narrower than it looked) is gone everywhere (see the numbers above); the Bierzelt (P09, the only `tent`) is walked into in the flow.

## 6. "Make the attractions rideable from the view from above"
- The manage sheet of a built attraction shows **🎢 Fueren** when `ride.canRide(i)` (seat rides, coasters, flume, ghost train, bumper cars, the wheel; not stalls, not during the tutorial or a visit). It calls `hooks.rideFromBuilder(i)` -> `walk.rideFrom(i)`: a black veil (`.wk-bfade`, 300 ms), the walk starts in front of the ride (the wheel: its boarding platform) with the screen kept black, the existing ride program boards, the ride's own fade opens. When the ride ends or is aborted (Esc, the red button) `ride.finish` calls `walk.leaveToBuilder()`: black, `walk.end()`, veil off. **The builder camera is restored exactly** (`end()` now freezes the rig at the view that was left instead of resuming a flight that was still easing: position 0 / orientation 0 after a settled view, the flow checks 1e-3 / 1e-4).
