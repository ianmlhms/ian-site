## SQL: `scripts/fouer-v2.sql` (idempotent, written, not run)
1. `game_gifts_game_id_check` widened with `'park'`; `admin_gift_live` and `claim_game_gifts` re-created as exact copies of the live definitions (read with `pg_get_functiondef` on 23 Oct) plus `'park'`. `admin_gift_idle` (the closed-game gift, it also has an allow-list) too, and it learns the v2 save: cash is `c`, lifetime is `tb`, and `rb` (the pioneer rating bonus) is NOT bumped, no `savedAt` is added. Without that, a closed-game gift to a park save would have failed with "unexpected save format" (the old code maps every other game to `p`).
2. `update scores set game_name='Schueberfouer Tycoon' where game_id='park'` (the column is `scores.game_name`).
3. Pioneer stubs `{"v":2,"stub":1,"pv":<score>}` for every `scores` row of `park` without a `game_saves` row, `on conflict (user_id, game_id) do nothing`.
4. A read-only verification select at the end.

Read-only facts checked on the live database (23 Oct 2026): 33 `scores` rows for `park` (33 users, best 13,362, lowest 6,200, all with `game_name='Theme Park Tycoon'`), **0** `game_saves` rows for `park` (so 33 stubs), `game_saves` columns `user_id, game_id, data jsonb, updated_at`, the only triggers are `game_saves_history` (AFTER UPDATE) and `game_saves_keep_progress` (BEFORE UPDATE; it only compares a numeric `tb`, a stub has none, so the first real v2 save over a stub passes; inserts are untouched), check `game_saves_data_check pg_column_size(data) <= 8192` (a stub is about 60 bytes), `game_gifts_game_id_check` = the 3 idle games, `client_commands_action_check` already has `gift`. The three functions with an allow-list: `admin_gift_idle`, `admin_gift_live`, `claim_game_gifts` (`ack_game_gift`, `admin_game_gifts`, `admin_cancel_gift` have none). `park_showcase`: 4 rows, all v1 (`{"version":1`), the newest written 21 Oct, ratings 0 to 76. The SELECT parts of the stub insert and of the verification were run read-only and return what is expected (`pv` 13362, 13127, 11628, ...).

## Events (`events.js`, DESIGN 6)
All driven by `game.events` (calendar.js) and the wall clock; the show clock is `seconds since Lux midnight - show start`, so every client agrees. `showAt(events, nowMs)` is pure and harness-tested with the real calendar (17:00:00 live, 17:03:00 over, 11:00 and 15:00 on Fouersonndeg only, 22:00 to 22:04 on the last day only, nothing outside the fair).
- **Opening ceremony** (opening day 17:00, 3 min): ribbon across the alley at the SW gate (u 30.2), mayor with scissors, a shepherd with Hämmi walking in from the east, a band of 8, 15 spectators, confetti and a fanfare at the cut (1:52), the ribbon halves drop. Captions in 4 phases. After 17:03 it is a skippable replay: settings menu > "Ouverture nach eng Kéier kucken" (shown once the ceremony is over).
- **Hämmelsmarsch** (Fouersonndeg 11:00 and 15:00, 205 s): 5 shepherds in blue smocks, straw hats and red neckerchiefs (crooks, one with a red collection box), 8 sheep with red-white-light-blue ribbons, a drum major and a dark-blue brass band of 8 (2 drums), walking the Haaptallee from the SW gate to the NE portal at 1.55 m/s. The calendar's 3 minutes are the head of the parade; the tail needs 205 s to leave the field, so the show runs until then.
- **Closing fireworks** (last day 22:00, 4 min, `fireworksSchedule(year)` seeded by the year, so identical everywhere): launched from the Red Bridge (the deck is found from the `rouge` landmark's geometry, principal axis and extent), 5 shell kinds (peony, willow, ring, crackle, double), a body of volleys and a 30 s finale, 7000-spark pool (one additive `Points`, CPU-animated). Weather and time stay the player's: only for the show the sky is turned to 22:24 (a 5 s time-lapse) and back to the player's time mode afterwards (`random`, `live`, a named time or a frozen hour all restore correctly); a sky that is already night is left alone; a second show while the sky is going back turns round from where it is.
- **Cinema camera**: the builder camera looks down at the ground and cannot see a sky or faces, so "Kuck zou" (the pills in the banner row, the replay) switches to a free camera that frames the show: the gate for the ceremony (outside the gate on the alley axis, drifting in), a dolly in front of the parade, a spot among the stalls looking at the sky over the bridge for the fireworks. "Zréck" gives the builder camera back (the show goes on), "Iwwersprangen" ends it. Walking (the 👣 button) ends the cinema first. During a cinema the HUD overlays (coin pops, queue clocks, goal) are hidden.
- **Banners**: a cloth banner over the Haaptallee at the gate (u 36, one cloth per side so the text never mirrors): "Familljendag" (Wednesdays), "Lescht Dag" (last day until 20:00), "Fouersonndeg". Text in the player's language.
- **Look**: figures are 3 instanced meshes (boxes, balls, cylinders) = 3 draw calls for any number of actors, lit by the scene's own lights (plus a little emissive at night), so they look right at any chosen weather and time. Their style follows the crowd's blocky figures (own geometry, `crowd.js` has no exported figure builder; `figureArrays` is skinned in a vertex shader that is not exported).
- **Sound** (WebAudio through `audio.context` and `audio.sfxBus`, nothing downloaded; silent while the arcade is muted or the context has not been started by a tap): a brass march (two detuned saws through a low-pass with a brass attack, tuba on the beats, off-beat chord stabs, snare and bass drum), positional (a PannerNode follows the band), a fanfare at the ribbon cut, launch whooshes, bangs and crackle. **The march is my own tune in the style of a Hämmelsmarsch, NOT the traditional notes.** I found the IMSLP arrangement (a CC BY-NC-SA file) but its download is behind a bot check, which I did not get around, and I do not know the melody reliably from memory. `MARCH_TUNE` in `events.js` is one small table (`[midi, beats]`, 32 beats, 120 bpm, Bb major): replace it with the real melody (a public-domain folk tune) and nothing else changes.
- **Test override**: `?fouerDate=2027-08-20T17:00:30+02:00` moves the clock, ONLY in test mode (main.js, unchanged); `__fouerGame.events.{debugShow(kind, seconds), stopDebug, state, watch, replay}` with `?fouerDebug=1`.

## Deploy order
**SQL first, then the deploy.** The SQL only adds (a wider check, allow-lists with one more game, a display name, stubs) and is safe for the old site. The other way round, (1) a pioneer who opens the new game before the SQL ran gets a normal `game_saves` row and the stub can no longer be inserted for them (`on conflict do nothing`), so they lose their migration bonus, and (2) the first live gift to `park` would fail with "game not allowed".
