A little land. A big imagination.

  1. Choose Build → Paths. Extend a path from the golden entrance gate into the light green land.
  2. Choose a ride, then tap an empty tile. Green means it fits; red explains what needs to change. Tap Build here to place it.
  3. The white dot marks an attraction's entrance. Put a connected path directly beside it. Rotate before building to move the entrance.
  4. Add food, drinks, toilets, and scenery. Tap any attraction to inspect, price, repair, or demolish it.
  5. Use Manage to hire staff and change prices. Follow Goals for cash rewards and new rides.

Drag to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Center frames everything you built. Walk lets you explore at guest height. Pause gives you time to build. Use Paths → Expand land to buy a neighboring parcel.
