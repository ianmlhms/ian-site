## Grafik levels (28 Nov 2026; same fix as Ski Alpen, see pb/ski/HANDOFF.md "Grafik levels")
- **Root cause**: main.js had a frame-rate watchdog that ignored the player's pick (only `?debug=1` / `?q=` turned it off), compared the per-second frame time with ABSOLUTE budgets (`FRAME_BUDGET_MS` high 24 / medium 30 / low 40 ms) and stepped down after 3 s above it, again and again. A school iPad at 30 fps (Low Power Mode caps requestAnimationFrame at 30 fps; an A13 at Héich is GPU-bound near that) has 33 ms frames: over Héich's and Mëttel's budget, so a picked Héich fell to Niddreg within ~6-8 s; the settings still said "Héich". (MTB has no dynamic resolution / post.js: the pixel ratio is part of the level, so there was no "resolution to the floor first" stage, and Niddreg is already 1.0 x, the same floor Ski got.)
- **Now**: the watchdog is gone. `pb/gfx-ctl/quality-ctl.js` (shared with Ski Alpen, in both import maps via `scripts/game_importmap.py`; unit test `node scripts/ski/quality-ctl-test.mjs`) decides. A manual pick (Niddreg / Mëttel / Héich in the settings) is final: at most one hint toast per tab ("Ze lues? Probéier Mëttel oder Automatesch", sessionStorage `pb_mtb_gfx_hint`), never a switch. Automatesch steps ONE level at a time with hysteresis (sustained slow median 4 s, >= 8 s between changes, back up after 15 s on the beat, failed up steps back off); where it settled is kept per device (localStorage `pb_mtb_gfx_auto`) and is the start level next time. Not judged: 5 s after the load, 6 s after a ride / free-ride start, 3 s after a reset / respawn, 5 s after every level change; card, countdown, menus, finish and replay frames only teach the display's beat (30 / 60 / 120 Hz, target = max(1/60 s, beat)). Crash recovery (WebGL context lost while visible: `pb_mtb_gfx_recover`; a page that died while riding: `pb_mtb_alive`) starts the next load ONE level lower with the toast "Grafik dës Kéier: <level> (no engem Feeler)"; the pick is never changed. `?q=` (tests) still switches everything off.
- **Look**: unchanged (no level's settings changed). Debug (`?debug=1`): `__mtb.gfx()`, `__mtb.slow(ms)` (adds ms to every judged frame). The debug flag no longer disables the controller (only `?q=` does).
- **Flow** `mtb-quality` (Chromium): Héich manual stays Héich 40 s under +30 ms frames (one hint, no switch), reload keeps it, crash recovery = one level lower for one load, Automatesch high -> medium -> low with a gap, back up when frames are fine. WebKit untested (Playwright WebKit broken on this Mac 28 Nov).
