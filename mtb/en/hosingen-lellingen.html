The MTB Hosingen - Lellingen is a 21.4 km mountain-bike loop around Hosingen - Lellingen in the Éislek, Luxembourg's north. It has 300 m of climbing in total and stays between 310 and 515 m above sea level. Riding time at a steady pace is about 2½ hours. Apart from Hosingen - Lellingen, it passes Bockholtz, Pintsch, Lellingen and Hosingen, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 59% of the distance is on tarmac and 41% on farm and forest tracks. 47% of the route runs through woodland. For about 2.5 km it runs close to the Lëllgerbaach. For about 0.8 km it runs close to the Pëntsch. It also crosses the Weschbichsbaach, Clerve and Helbichbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 147 m between km 15 and km 20.6; the steepest 500 m, from km 19.2, average 6%. The highest point, 515 m, comes at km 26.6.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Vue Kiischpelt (viewpoint, 279 m from the route), Geesselee (viewpoint, 360 m from the route) and Lellingen - Freng / Op Baerel (nature reserve, 385 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Maximin, Chapelle Saint-Pierre and Schmärzensmuttergottes-Kapell. OpenStreetMap lists 8 benches, 5 picnic spots, 5 shelters, 3 playgrounds and a drinking-water tap within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Dragon de Chine (restaurant, km 29.9) and M.M Restaurant (km 29.9).

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest loops it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 300 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, Mountainbike / VTT Tour 05 Hosingen (4.3 km away), is 12.7 km long with 240 m of climbing. For a longer ride: MTB Goesdorf, 35.7 km.
