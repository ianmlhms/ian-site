The Becher mini Bike is a 1.6 km mountain-bike loop around Bech in the Mullerthal region. It is almost flat: the whole route stays between 351 and 379 m above sea level. Riding time at a steady pace is about ½ hour. Apart from Bech, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 27% of the distance is on farm and forest tracks and 73% on singletrack and narrow paths. 97% of the route runs through woodland. The difference between the highest and the lowest point is only 28 m.

Along the way

OpenStreetMap lists 2 benches, a picnic spot and a shelter within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Becher Gare (restaurant, km 0.2).

When to go

With 97% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 100% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; expect slippery sections.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest loops, this one is the shortest, has the least climbing, has the most woodland and has the least tarmac. The closest, MTB Bech (1.5 km away), is 13.3 km long with 160 m of climbing.
