Mountain-bike loops in the Minett

The Minett in the south is Luxembourg's former iron-ore district. Part of the old open-cast mining land is now nature reserve.

4 loops from 28 to 32.7 km, 118 km in total. 4 of them have a bus stop right on the route.

Sorted by distance:

  3. MTB RedRock Ellergrond28.9 km · 250 m climbing · approx. 3 h · 64% woodland
