The MTB Clervaux is a 24.8 km mountain-bike loop around Clervaux in the Éislek, Luxembourg's north. It has 390 m of climbing in total and stays between 350 and 512 m above sea level. Riding time at a steady pace is about 3 hours. Apart from Clervaux, it passes Mecher, Reuler, Urspelt and Maulesmillen, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 62% of the distance is on tarmac, 35% on farm and forest tracks and 3% on singletrack and narrow paths. 44% of the route runs through woodland. For about 3.1 km it runs close to the Clerve. For about 1.7 km it runs close to the Woltz. There is one flight of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 131 m between km 7.6 and km 13; the steepest 500 m, from km 17, average 7%. The highest point, 512 m, comes at km 13.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Villa Berlaymont (castle, 34 m from the route), Château de Clervaux (castle, 88 m from the route), Musée de la Bataille des Ardennes (museum, 65 m from the route) and The Family of Man (museum, 75 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saints-Côme-et-Damien, Loretto Kapell and Saint-Maurice. OpenStreetMap lists 27 benches, 9 picnic spots, 2 shelters, 2 playgrounds and 2 public toilets within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: La Table de Clervaux (restaurant, km 4.3), Kebab Nord (restaurant, km 4.3), Openrice (restaurant, km 4.7) and Les écuries du parc (restaurant, km 5).

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest loops it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 390 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, MTB Strecke (7.5 km away), is 6.8 km long with 230 m of climbing.
