# NOTE: the private / sign-in-only pages (admin, factory, homework, hotel,
# karts, kart, messenger, friends, exams, profile, health, money, countdowns,

# as well meant Google never saw the noindex, so they could sit in the index as
# bare URL entries indefinitely — the opposite of the intent, and thin entries
# count against the site in an AdSense review.
#
# Crawling them leaks nothing: they are auth-gated and render almost no markup
# to a signed-out crawler.
