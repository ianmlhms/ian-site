KartTracker privacy policy

🗓️ Last updated: 15 October 2026

KartTracker is a free karting lap timer for Garmin watches, made by Ian Mulheims in Luxembourg as a hobby project. This page explains exactly what the app sends, who can see it and how to delete it. For the rest of ian.lu, see the general privacy policy.

👤 Who is responsible

Ian Mulheims, Luxembourg · Contact: konto@ian.lu

✨ In short

  • The watch only uploads a session when you end it and it has laps.
  • A session is visible to whoever has its link or QR code, and to no one else.
  • The watch never sends your name, email address or Garmin account.
  • No ads, no tracking in the app, nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your sessions at any time.

What the app sends

1 Session uploads

When you end a session that has laps, the watch sends it through the Garmin Connect app on your phone to ian.lu. A session contains:

DataWhy
Lap times, datethe lap timer itself
GPS track with speed (at most 600 points) and the start/finish line positionthe racing line on the map and the speed view
Average and maximum heart ratesession stats (only if your watch measures it)
A random session ID, the app versionthe share link and fixing bugs
A random watch code (see 2)linking the watch to an account

The session link (ian.lu/kart.html?s=…) contains a long random ID. Anyone you give the link or QR code to can view the session; it is not listed or searchable anywhere. As the site operator, Ian can see all sessions to run and fix the service.

2 Watch code and karting account (optional)

On first start the watch creates a random 128-bit code and keeps it on the watch. It is sent with every upload, and ian.lu stores only a one-way hash of it. It identifies the watch, not you.

If you scan the link QR code (hold MENU) and sign in, the watch is linked to your ian.lu account. A karting account needs an email address, a password and a username; the username is shown on the watch. From then on, sessions from that watch are saved to your account, together with the earlier sessions that watch uploaded. On My karting you can rename and delete sessions and unlink watches.

3 What stays on Garmin

The activity itself (with laps) is recorded by your watch and synced to Garmin Connect as usual. That part is handled by Garmin under Garmin's privacy policy; KartTracker does not receive your Garmin data.

4 Where it is stored

Sessions and accounts are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). The session pages count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data.

🗑️ Keeping and deleting data

Sessions are kept until you delete them. Sessions in your account can be deleted on My karting. To delete your whole karting account, use Delete account on My karting (for a full ian.lu account, write to konto@ian.lu); deleting an account deletes all its sessions. For a session that is not in an account, send its link to the same address and it will be deleted.

🛡️ Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and you can object to its processing. Write to konto@ian.lu. You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority (CNPD).

🧒 Children

KartTracker is not aimed at children under 16. If you are under 16, please only create a karting account with your parents' permission.
