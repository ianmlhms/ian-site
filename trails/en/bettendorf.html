This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Bettendorf in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The loop is 11.7 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3 hours. The route gathers about 170 m of ascent.

Worth a look en route: Château de Bettendorf and Ancienne Carrière de Bettendorf - Schoofsbësch. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
