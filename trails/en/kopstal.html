The Auto-Pédestre Kopstal is a 9.6 km loop walk around Kopstal in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 160 m of climbing in total and stays between 259 and 379 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Kopstal, the only village on the way is Direndall.

The route in detail

About 19% of the distance is on tarmac, 76% on farm and forest tracks and 5% on narrow paths. 76% of the route runs through woodland. It also crosses the Mamer. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 120 m between km 0 and km 4.5; the steepest 500 m, from km 3.3, average 7%. The highest point, 379 m, comes at km 4.5.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Missiounskraiz (viewpoint, 52 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Nicolas. OpenStreetMap lists 11 benches and 2 picnic spots within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Cafe-Restaurant op der Breck (km 0.1), Restaurant Weidendall (km 0.2) and Edelwyss (restaurant, km 9.3).

When to go

With 76% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 81% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the longest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Bridel (1.8 km away), is 8.2 km long with 110 m of climbing. For something shorter nearby: Auto-Pédestre Bereldange, 7.4 km.
