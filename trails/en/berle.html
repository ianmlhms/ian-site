Around Berlé the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The route measures 8.4 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 180 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
