Around Moesdorf, in central Luxembourg, you cross the Guttland: gentle hills, fields, stream valleys and stretches of forest. The route measures 7.5 km — about 2 hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 200 m of ascent.

On the route: Pompjeesmusee Gemeng Miersch. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
