Berdorf belongs to the Mullerthal region in Luxembourg's east — rocky passages, shaded woods and quiet field sections take turns on this loop. The loop is 6.6 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2 hours. There is barely any climbing.

Along the way you pass Perekop. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
