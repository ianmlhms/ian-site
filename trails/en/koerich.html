The Auto-Pédestre Koerich is a 7.4 km loop walk around Koerich in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 100 m of climbing in total and stays between 273 and 361 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Koerich, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 36% of the distance is on tarmac, 52% on farm and forest tracks and 12% on narrow paths. 41% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.7 km it runs close to the L'Eisch. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 57 m between km 0 and km 2.9; the steepest 500 m, from km 1.5, average 6%. The highest point, 361 m, comes at km 2.9.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Gréiveschlass (castle, 40 m from the route) and Koerich Castle (castle, 56 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Remi and Remigiussall. OpenStreetMap lists 12 benches and 3 picnic spots within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Asie Moderne (restaurant, km 0.1) and Villa d'Asie (restaurant, km 7.3).

When to go

64% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest and has the least woodland. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Septfontaines (2.8 km away), is 9.4 km long with 180 m of climbing.
