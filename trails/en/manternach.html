The Auto-Pédestre Manternach is a 10.4 km loop walk around Manternach in the Mullerthal region. It has 170 m of climbing in total and stays between 198 and 318 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 3 hours. Apart from Manternach, the only village on the way is Lellig.

The route in detail

About 40% of the distance is on tarmac, 41% on farm and forest tracks and 19% on narrow paths. 27% of the route runs through woodland. For about 1.4 km it runs close to the Syre. It also crosses the Wuelbertsbach. There are 7 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 89 m between km 1.8 and km 4.5; the steepest 500 m, from km 2.1, average 7%. The highest point, 318 m, comes at km 5.7.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Laerensmillen (ruins, 196 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Antoine-l'Ermite and Saint-Brice. OpenStreetMap lists 6 benches, 4 picnic spots, 3 shelters and a drinking-water tap within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Belleville (restaurant, km 0) and Manternacher Stuff (café, km 10.1).

When to go

60% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest circuits it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 170 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Mertert (1.9 km away), is 10.7 km long with 200 m of climbing. For something shorter nearby: Auto-Pédestre Wasserbillig, 7.9 km.
