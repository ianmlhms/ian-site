Manternach belongs to the Mullerthal region in Luxembourg's east — rocky passages, shaded woods and quiet field sections take turns on this loop. The loop is 10.4 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3 hours. The route gathers about 170 m of ascent.

Along the way you pass Laerensmillen. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
