This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Hoffelt in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The route measures 9.8 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 130 m of ascent.

The route mostly follows near-natural paths through woods and open country. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
