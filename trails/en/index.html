Luxembourg's Auto-Pédestre loop trails

The Auto-Pédestres are Luxembourg's classic circular walks: 154 local loops across the country, permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and always bringing you back to where you started. Many were laid out decades ago for day-trippers arriving by car, hence the name, but today most are easy to reach by bus, since public transport is free throughout Luxembourg.

This site presents the trails one by one, with an interactive map, distance, approximate walking time, difficulty and the bus stops right on the route. The list follows the official register on geoportail.lu, which was cut from 201 circuits to today's 154, so everything here is still waymarked on the ground. Route lines come from OpenStreetMap and from the Land Registry and Topography Administration's open data, stop data from Luxembourg's official timetable feed.
