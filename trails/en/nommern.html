Around Nommern, in central Luxembourg, you cross the Guttland: gentle hills, fields, stream valleys and stretches of forest. At 5.6 km, the circuit takes roughly 1½ hours. The route gathers about 140 m of ascent.

On the route: Nommerlayen. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
