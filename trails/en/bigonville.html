This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Bigonville in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The loop is 12.3 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3½ hours. The route gathers about 230 m of ascent.

On the route: Millefiels. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
