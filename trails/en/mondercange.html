The Auto-Pédestre Mondercange is a 7.2 km loop walk around Mondercange in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 70 m of climbing in total and stays between 293 and 347 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Mondercange, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 71% of the distance is on tarmac and 28% on farm and forest tracks. 24% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.5 km it runs close to the Kazebaach. It also crosses the Bréckenderbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 51 m between km 1.6 and km 5.9. The highest point, 347 m, comes at km 5.9.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Wolfswiss (nature reserve, 57 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Willibrord. OpenStreetMap lists 22 benches, 5 picnic spots, 2 shelters and 3 playgrounds within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Rugova (restaurant, km 2.6) and Restaurant le Colibri Bleu (km 8.7).

When to go

As most of it is tarmac, it stays easy going after rain.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Belvaux (3.9 km away), is 9.4 km long with 170 m of climbing.
