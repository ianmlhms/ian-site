This Auto-Pédestre explores the area around Echternach / Lac in the Mullerthal region, where sandstone, forest and small streams shape the landscape. At 7.4 km, the circuit takes roughly 2 hours. The route gathers about 190 m of ascent.

On the route, among others: Lëschenhaus and Réimer Villa Echternach. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
