The Auto-Pédestre Echternach / Lac is a 7.4 km loop walk around Echternach / Lac in the Mullerthal region. It has 190 m of climbing in total and stays between 171 and 365 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Echternach / Lac, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 35% of the distance is on tarmac, 39% on farm and forest tracks and 21% on narrow paths. 63% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.4 km it runs close to the Lauterburerbaach. It also crosses the Lëschtebaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 189 m between km 0 and km 2.4; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.2, average 15%. The highest point, 365 m, comes at km 2.4.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Lëschenhaus (ruins, 121 m from the route), Réimer Villa Echternach (museum, 47 m from the route) and Villa Romana (museum, 66 m from the route). OpenStreetMap lists 23 benches, 4 picnic spots, a shelter, 4 playgrounds and 2 public toilets within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Lakeside (restaurant, km 0.1).

When to go

With 63% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 60% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest circuits it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 190 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Echternach 2 / Gare (2.3 km away), is 5.3 km long with 160 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Osweiler, 11.8 km.
