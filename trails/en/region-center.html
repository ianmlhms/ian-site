Auto-Pédestres in the Guttland

The Guttland covers the centre and the west of the country, with fields, forests and the valleys of the Alzette, the Attert and the Eisch.

68 loops from 4.3 to 12.3 km, 565 km in total. 60 of them have a bus stop right on the route.
