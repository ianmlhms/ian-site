This roughly eleven-kilometre loop explores the Senningerberg plateau in the commune of Niederanven, between the edge of the Gréngewald forest and the open Syre valley. The route alternates pleasantly between woodland, farm tracks and village-edge paths with wide views over the Syrdall.

Although Findel airport sits right next door, most of the route runs through surprisingly quiet fields and woods. The forest-edge sections show how quickly you move from built-up areas into genuine countryside here, typical of the communes east of the capital.

At this length the loop makes a solid half-day walk, yet it stays free of technical difficulty. It is well served by bus, and because it is a circuit you can start it from several points.

The Auto-Pédestre Senningerberg is a 11 km loop walk around Niederanven in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 110 m of climbing in total and stays between 370 and 420 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 3 hours. Apart from Niederanven, it passes Senningerberg and Rameldange, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 13% of the distance is on tarmac, 78% on farm and forest tracks and 9% on narrow paths. 89% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.4 km it runs close to the Iernsterbaach. There is one flight of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 50 m between km 1.9 and km 3.6; the steepest 500 m, from km 7.6, average 4%. The highest point, 420 m, comes at km 3.6.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Lorettokapelle. OpenStreetMap lists 9 benches, 5 picnic spots and a shelter within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Porta Vecchia (restaurant, km 0.1) and Charlie's Gare (restaurant, km 0.1).

When to go

With 89% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 87% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the longest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Gréngewald (1.6 km away), is 4.9 km long with 80 m of climbing.
