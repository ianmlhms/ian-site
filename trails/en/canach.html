Around Canach, vineyards and views over the Moselle valley set the scene — the loop links vine slopes, plateau tracks and quiet side valleys. The route measures 12.9 km — about 3½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 190 m of ascent.

Field and forest tracks alternate with quiet back roads. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
