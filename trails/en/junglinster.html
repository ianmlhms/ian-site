Around Junglinster, in central Luxembourg, you cross the Guttland: gentle hills, fields, stream valleys and stretches of forest. At 9.1 km, the circuit takes roughly 2½ hours. The route gathers about 130 m of ascent.

Field and forest tracks alternate with quiet back roads. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
