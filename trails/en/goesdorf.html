This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Goesdorf in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. At 6.8 km, the circuit takes roughly 2 hours. The route gathers about 200 m of ascent.

Along the way you pass Antimon-Musée. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
