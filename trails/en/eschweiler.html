The Auto-Pédestre Eschweiler is a 7 km loop walk around Eschweiler in the Éislek, Luxembourg's north. It has 140 m of climbing in total and stays between 372 and 485 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Eschweiler, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 46% of the distance is on tarmac, 50% on farm and forest tracks and 4% on narrow paths. 35% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.8 km it runs close to the Burebaach. For about 0.7 km it runs close to the Ierpeldéngerbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 113 m between km 1.1 and km 4.8; the steepest 500 m, from km 4.2, average 6%. The highest point, 485 m, comes at km 4.8.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Maurice. OpenStreetMap lists 5 benches, a picnic spot, a shelter and a playground within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: An der Gaessen (restaurant, km 0).

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest and has the least climbing. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Wiltz (3.6 km away), is 13.5 km long with 320 m of climbing.
