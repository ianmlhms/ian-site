This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Tadler in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The loop is 4.5 km long; at a normal pace expect about 1½ hours. The route gathers about 150 m of ascent.

The route mostly follows near-natural paths through woods and open country. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
