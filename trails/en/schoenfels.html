Around Schoenfels, in central Luxembourg, you cross the Guttland: gentle hills, fields, stream valleys and stretches of forest. At 9.7 km, the circuit takes roughly 3 hours. The route gathers about 220 m of ascent.

On the route, among others: Wehrturm Schlass Schëndels and Château de Schoenfels. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
