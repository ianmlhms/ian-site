This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Büderscheid in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. At 4.6 km, the circuit takes roughly 1½ hours. The route gathers about 130 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
