The Auto-Pédestre Moersdorf is a 6.9 km loop walk around Moersdorf in the Mullerthal region. It has 220 m of climbing in total and stays between 158 and 328 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Moersdorf, it passes Metzdorf and Givenich, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 23% of the distance is on tarmac, 59% on farm and forest tracks and 17% on narrow paths. 58% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.9 km it runs close to the Kauzebaach. It also crosses the Baach. There are 3 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 141 m between km 0 and km 1.3; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.2, average 14%. The highest point, 328 m, comes at km 2.5.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Réserve Naturelle Deiwelskopp (nature reserve, 55 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Chapelle de Givenich and Mäerteskierch. OpenStreetMap lists 4 benches, a picnic spot and 2 shelters within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Sakura House (restaurant, km 0).

When to go

With 58% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 76% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Born (2.6 km away), is 9 km long with 140 m of climbing.
