The Auto-Pédestre Kehlen is a 8.8 km loop walk around Kehlen in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 140 m of climbing in total and stays between 294 and 371 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Kehlen, it passes Weidendall and Brameschhaff, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 42% of the distance is on tarmac, 51% on farm and forest tracks and 7% on narrow paths. 56% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.5 km it runs close to the Rielbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 69 m between km 4.7 and km 5.8; the steepest 500 m, from km 4.9, average 9%. The highest point, 371 m, comes at km 0.9.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Maximin and D´Moelleschkapell. OpenStreetMap lists 7 benches and 2 playgrounds within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: La Lambretta (restaurant, km 8.6) and Restaurant Kai Yue (km 8.7).

When to go

With 56% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days.

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest circuits it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 140 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Mamer Thillsmillen (1.7 km away), is 7.7 km long with 110 m of climbing.
