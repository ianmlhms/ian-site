The Auto-Pédestre Scheidgen is a 7.5 km loop walk around Scheidgen in the Mullerthal region. It has 90 m of climbing in total and stays between 310 and 361 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Scheidgen, it passes Crispent and Juckenfeld, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 27% of the distance is on tarmac, 27% on farm and forest tracks and 46% on narrow paths. 84% of the route runs through woodland. For about 3.4 km it runs close to the Lauterburerbaach. It also crosses the Kalkesbaach. There are 5 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 40 m between km 0 and km 1.2; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.1, average 5%. The highest point, 361 m, comes at km 1.2.

Along the way

OpenStreetMap lists 13 benches, 6 picnic spots, 3 shelters and a playground within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: The Grill (restaurant, km 0) and Daisy (restaurant, km 2.9).

When to go

With 84% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 73% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Among the 5 nearest circuits it is neither the longest nor the shortest, and its 90 m of climbing are not an extreme either. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Consdorf-Müllerthal (3.1 km away), is 9.3 km long with 190 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Bech, 10.6 km.
