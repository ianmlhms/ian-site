This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Mertzig in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. At 8.6 km, the circuit takes roughly 2½ hours. The route gathers about 150 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
