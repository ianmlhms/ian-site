Septfontaines lies in the Guttland — quiet farm tracks, woodland passages and village edges give this loop its rhythm. The loop is 9.4 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2½ hours. The route gathers about 180 m of ascent.

On the route: Château de Septfontaines. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
