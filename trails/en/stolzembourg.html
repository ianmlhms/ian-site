Around Stolzembourg the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The route measures 7.6 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 220 m of ascent.

Worth a look en route: Buerg Stolzebuerg and Koffergrouf vu Stolzebuerg. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
