Near Osweiler you walk on the edge of the Mullerthal region, Luxembourg's 'Little Switzerland' of sandstone rocks and wooded stream valleys. The loop is 11.8 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3½ hours. The route gathers about 210 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
