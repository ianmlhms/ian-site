The Auto-Pédestre Rodange is a 7 km loop walk around Rodange in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 120 m of climbing in total and stays between 286 and 385 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Rodange, the only village on the way is Fond-de-Gras.

The route in detail

About 27% of the distance is on tarmac, 40% on farm and forest tracks and 33% on narrow paths. 71% of the route runs through woodland. There are 2 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 99 m between km 0 and km 3.4; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.5, average 9%. The highest point, 385 m, comes at km 3.4.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Mirador de Lamadelaine (viewpoint, 35 m from the route), Epicerie Binck (museum, 10 m from the route), Hall Paul Wurth (museum, 45 m from the route) and Parc Industriel et Ferroviaire du Fond-de-Gras (museum, 178 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Église Sainte-Amalberge. OpenStreetMap lists 25 benches, a picnic spot, 3 playgrounds and 2 public toilets within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Bei der Giedel Fond-de-Gras (restaurant, km 2.8), Champs de Roses (restaurant, km 6.2) and Pokhara (restaurant, km 6.9).

When to go

With 71% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 73% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest and has the least tarmac. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Lamadelaine (1.5 km away), is 10.8 km long with 130 m of climbing.
