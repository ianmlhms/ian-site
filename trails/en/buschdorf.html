The Auto-Pédestre Buschdorf is a 7.7 km loop walk around Buschdorf in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 180 m of climbing in total and stays between 268 and 398 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Buschdorf, it passes Openthalt, Finsterthal and Grevenknapp, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 24% of the distance is on tarmac, 60% on farm and forest tracks and 15% on narrow paths. 70% of the route runs through woodland. It also crosses the Kandelbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 126 m between km 0 and km 1.9; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.6, average 9%. The highest point, 398 m, comes at km 1.9.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Fensterdall Zone B (nature reserve, 50 m from the route) and Fensterdall Zone A (nature reserve, 162 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Jean-Baptiste and Chapelle Saint-Willibrord. OpenStreetMap lists 7 benches, 5 picnic spots, a shelter and a drinking-water tap within 60 m of the route. There is no restaurant or café directly on the route.

When to go

With 70% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 75% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the shortest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Brouch (2.3 km away), is 9.1 km long with 220 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Useldange, 10.8 km.
