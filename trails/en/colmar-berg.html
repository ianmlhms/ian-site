The Auto-Pédestre Colmar-Berg is a 7.2 km loop walk around Colmar-Berg in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 150 m of climbing in total and stays between 205 and 344 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2 hours. Apart from Colmar-Berg, it passes Berg and Colmar, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 36% of the distance is on tarmac and 64% on farm and forest tracks. 72% of the route runs through woodland. For about 2 km it runs close to the Schwaarzbaach. For about 0.7 km it runs close to the L'Attert. It also crosses the Helmeschbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 139 m between km 0 and km 4.6; the steepest 500 m, from km 1.2, average 6%. The highest point, 344 m, comes at km 4.6.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Château de Berg (castle, 162 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Michel. OpenStreetMap lists 9 benches, a picnic spot, a shelter, a playground, a drinking-water tap and a public toilet within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Am Park (restaurant, km 0.1).

When to go

With 72% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 64% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one has the least climbing and has the most woodland. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Schieren (2.5 km away), is 7 km long with 190 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Ettelbruck 2, 9.3 km.
