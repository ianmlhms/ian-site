This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Bourscheid in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The route measures 5.8 km — about 2 hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 210 m of ascent.

On the route, among others: Buurschter Schlass and Schlaedbësch. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
