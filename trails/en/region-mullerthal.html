Auto-Pédestres in the Mullerthal

The Mullerthal region lies in the east, on the German border. It is known for sandstone rocks and wooded stream valleys, above all along the White and the Black Ernz.

15 loops from 5.3 to 12.7 km, 134 km in total. 14 of them have a bus stop right on the route.
