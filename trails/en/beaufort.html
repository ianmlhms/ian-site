Around Beaufort the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The loop is 8.7 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2½ hours. The route gathers about 120 m of ascent.

On the route, among others: Buerg Beefort and Schlass Beefort. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
