Around Oberdonven, vineyards and views over the Moselle valley set the scene — the loop links vine slopes, plateau tracks and quiet side valleys. The route measures 10 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 140 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
