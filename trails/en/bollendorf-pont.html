This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Bollendorf-Pont in the Éislek, Luxembourg's hilly north of quiet forests and deeply cut river valleys. The loop is 8.7 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2½ hours. The route gathers about 200 m of ascent.

Field and forest tracks alternate with quiet back roads. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
