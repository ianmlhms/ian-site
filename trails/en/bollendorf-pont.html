The Auto-Pédestre Bollendorf-Pont is a 8.7 km loop walk around Bollendorf-Pont in the Éislek, Luxembourg's north. It has 200 m of climbing in total and stays between 169 and 382 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Bollendorf-Pont, it passes Weilerbach and Lafewier, in the order of the GPX track.

The route in detail

About 41% of the distance is on tarmac, 15% on farm and forest tracks and 44% on narrow paths. 77% of the route runs through woodland. For about 3.4 km it runs close to the Sauer - Sûre. There are 7 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 187 m between km 0 and km 1.6; the steepest 500 m, from km 0.7, average 16%. The highest point, 382 m, comes at km 1.6.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Evangelisch Kirche Bollendorf and Mariä Himmelfaart. OpenStreetMap lists 24 benches, 4 picnic spots and a shelter within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Burg Bollendorf (restaurant, km 7.8), Hotel Hauer (restaurant, km 8.4) and La Véranda (café, km 8.6).

When to go

With 77% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one has the most woodland and has the most tarmac. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Berdorf (2.3 km away), is 6.6 km long with 80 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Grundhof, 11.1 km.
