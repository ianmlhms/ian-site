Rambrouch lies in the Guttland — quiet farm tracks, woodland passages and village edges give this loop its rhythm. The loop is 10.8 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3 hours. The route gathers about 170 m of ascent.

On small paths, the loop links the prettiest corners around the village. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
