Around Vianden the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The route measures 7.9 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. With some 320 m of climbing, the loop is distinctly hilly.

On the route, among others: Château de Vianden and Musée de la Ville de Vianden et du Jouet. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
