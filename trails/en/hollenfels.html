This Auto-Pédestre explores the surroundings of Hollenfels in the Guttland, the varied heart of the country between fields and woods. The loop is 8.5 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2½ hours. The route gathers about 130 m of ascent.

Along the way you pass Château de Hollenfels. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
