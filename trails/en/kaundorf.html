The Auto-Pédestre Kaundorf is a 8.5 km loop walk around Kaundorf in the Éislek, Luxembourg's north. It has 140 m of climbing in total and stays between 373 and 467 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Kaundorf, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 17% of the distance is on tarmac, 60% on farm and forest tracks and 22% on narrow paths. 53% of the route runs through woodland. It also crosses the Miineschbaach. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 80 m between km 5.8 and km 8.5; the steepest 500 m, from km 6.6, average 5%. The highest point, 467 m, comes at km 2.7.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Chapelle Saint-Pirmin and Haupeschkierch. OpenStreetMap lists 16 benches, 8 picnic spots, a shelter, 2 playgrounds and a public toilet within 60 m of the route. There is no restaurant or café directly on the route.

When to go

With 53% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 82% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one has the least tarmac. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Esch-sur-Sûre (1.6 km away), is 9.5 km long with 250 m of climbing. For something shorter nearby: Auto-Pédestre Büderscheid, 4.6 km. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Wiltz, 13.5 km.
