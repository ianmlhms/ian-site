Around Harlange the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The loop is 8.3 km long; at a normal pace expect about 2½ hours. The route gathers about 140 m of ascent.

Field and forest tracks alternate with quiet back roads. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
