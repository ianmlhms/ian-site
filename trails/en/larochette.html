The Auto-Pédestre Larochette is a 8.5 km loop walk around Larochette in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 180 m of climbing in total and stays between 279 and 353 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Larochette, there is no other village on the way.

The route in detail

About 21% of the distance is on tarmac, 19% on farm and forest tracks and 60% on narrow paths. 74% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.7 km it runs close to the Wäiss Iernz. It also crosses the Ernz Blanche. There are 9 flights of steps on the way. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 67 m between km 0 and km 2.6; the steepest 500 m, from km 6.6, average 10%. The highest point, 353 m, comes at km 2.6.

Along the way

Within a few hundred metres: Verluerekascht (castle, 19 m from the route), Buerg Fiels (castle, 85 m from the route), Hangelsbour (waterfall, 37 m from the route) and Himmelsbierg (viewpoint, 17 m from the route). Churches and chapels on the way: Grotte de Lourdes, Rochuskapell and Saint-Donat. OpenStreetMap lists 33 benches, 9 picnic spots, 6 shelters, 3 playgrounds, a drinking-water tap and a public toilet within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: D'Fielser Stuff (restaurant, km 0), Hua Ting (restaurant, km 0), Ginza (restaurant, km 7.6) and Café de la Place (km 8.4).

When to go

With 74% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 79% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one is the longest. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Nommern (3.1 km away), is 5.6 km long with 140 m of climbing.
