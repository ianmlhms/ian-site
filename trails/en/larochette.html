Larochette lies in the Guttland — quiet farm tracks, woodland passages and village edges give this loop its rhythm. The route measures 8.5 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 180 m of ascent.

On the route, among others: Verluerekascht and Buerg Fiels. The circuit carries the blue Auto-Pédestre waymark throughout; being a loop, it has no fixed start.
