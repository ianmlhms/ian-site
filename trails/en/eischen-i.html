The Auto-Pédestre Eischen I is a 7.7 km loop walk around Eischen in the Guttland, the centre of the country. It has 250 m of climbing in total and stays between 282 and 386 m above sea level. At a steady walking pace it takes about 2½ hours. Apart from Eischen, the only village on the way is Gaichel.

The route in detail

About 33% of the distance is on tarmac, 49% on farm and forest tracks and 18% on narrow paths. 70% of the route runs through woodland. For about 0.4 km it runs close to the Millebaach. It also crosses the L'Eisch, Bech and Geichel. In the direction of the GPX track, the longest climb gains 77 m between km 3.1 and km 4.1; the steepest 500 m, from km 3.2, average 10%. The highest point, 386 m, comes at km 7.1.

Along the way

Churches and chapels on the way: Saint-Pierre. OpenStreetMap lists 12 benches within 60 m of the route. Places to eat right by the route: Gudde Maufel (restaurant, km 0.1), La Terrazza (restaurant, km 1.8) and Aischener Stuff (restaurant, km 1.9).

When to go

With 70% woodland, much of the route is in shade, which helps on hot days. 67% of the route is unpaved and can be muddy after rain and in winter; sturdy shoes help.

Compared with the trails nearby

Compared with the 5 nearest circuits, this one has the most climbing. The closest, Auto-Pédestre Hobscheid (1.9 km away), is 8.7 km long with 170 m of climbing. For a longer walk: Auto-Pédestre Beckerich, 11.5 km.
