Around Binsfeld the Éislek shows its classic face: deep valleys, wooded ridges and wide views over Luxembourg's Ardennes. The route measures 8.8 km — about 2½ hours of walking, depending on pace. The route gathers about 120 m of ascent.

On the route: Musée Rural A Schiewesch. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
