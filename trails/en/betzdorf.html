This Auto-Pédestre tours the surroundings of Betzdorf on the Luxembourg Moselle, between vine rows, orchards and glimpses across to the German bank. The loop is 10 km long; at a normal pace expect about 3 hours. The route gathers about 170 m of ascent.

On the route: Château de Betzdorf. Like every Auto-Pédestre, the loop is permanently waymarked with the blue symbol and can be started at any point.
