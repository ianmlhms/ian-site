KartTracker for iPhone & Apple Watch: privacy policy

🗓️ Last updated: 25 October 2026

KartTracker is a free karting lap timer for Apple Watch with a companion iPhone app, made by Ian Mulheims in Luxembourg as a hobby project. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your devices and how to delete it. The Garmin version has its own policy; for the rest of ian.lu, see the general privacy policy.

👤 Who is responsible

Ian Mulheims, Luxembourg · Contact: konto@ian.lu

✨ In short

  • Your sessions, tracks, location and heart rate stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Something is uploaded only when you tap Share Web Link on a session, or when you link an optional karting account (then new sessions are saved to it).
  • A shared session is visible to whoever has its link, and to no one else. Stop Sharing deletes it.
  • No account needed, no ads, no analytics or tracking in the app, nothing is sold.
  • Health data is never used for advertising and never stored in iCloud by the app.

What the app uses

1 Location

The watch uses GPS only while a session is running, to detect when you cross the start/finish line and to draw your driven line on the map. The GPS track and the start/finish lines of your saved tracks are stored on your watch and iPhone.

2 Heart rate and Apple Health

If you allow it, the watch reads your heart rate during a session to show it live and to save the average and maximum with the session. The session is also saved as a workout in Apple Health, which keeps the app running while your wrist is down. Health data is handled by Apple on your devices; KartTracker does not send it anywhere unless you share a session (see 4).

3 Sync between your watch and iPhone

Finished sessions and saved tracks are copied between your own Apple Watch and iPhone with Apple's WatchConnectivity. This goes directly between your devices; it does not pass through ian.lu.

4 Sharing a web link (optional)

When you tap Share Web Link and confirm, the iPhone uploads that one session to ian.lu so it can be opened in a browser. A shared session contains:

Lap times, date, track namethe session page
GPS track with speed (at most 1,500 points)the racing line on the map and the speed view
Average and maximum heart ratesession stats (only if recorded)
A random share ID and a random edit keythe link, and making sure only your iPhone can update or delete it (ian.lu stores only a one-way hash of the key)

The link (ian.lu/kart.html?s=…) contains a long random ID. Anyone you give it to can view the session; it is not listed or searchable anywhere. As the site operator, Ian can see shared sessions to run and fix the service. Your name, email address and Apple Account are never sent.

5 Karting account (optional)

In Settings › Karting account you can link the app to a free ian.lu karting account, the same one the Garmin app uses. The app creates a random 128-bit code for your iPhone and keeps it in the iPhone's keychain; ian.lu stores only a one-way hash of it. Linking opens ian.lu/kart-link.html, where you sign in or create the account (email address, password and a username). The app then shows your username, also on the watch.

While linked, new sessions (the data listed in 4) are saved to your account automatically; you can turn that off, or save sessions one by one. On My karting you can rename and delete sessions, unlink devices and delete the account. Unlink this iPhone in the app stops new uploads and creates a new code; sessions already saved stay in the account.

6 Where shared sessions are stored

Shared sessions are stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). The session page counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal data.

🗑️ Keeping and deleting data

Data on your devices stays until you delete it: swipe to delete a session or lap in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. A shared session stays online until you tap Stop Sharing on it in the iPhone app. If you no longer have the iPhone it was shared from, send the link to konto@ian.lu and it will be deleted. Sessions in a karting account can be deleted on My karting, where you can also delete the whole account with all its sessions (Delete account).

🛡️ Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and you can object to its processing. Write to konto@ian.lu. You can also complain to the Luxembourg data protection authority (CNPD).

🧒 Children

KartTracker is not aimed at children under 16. If you are under 16, please ask your parents before sharing sessions or creating a karting account.
