The estate

Maison Viticole Roeder is a small organic estate in Steinheim, on the Hoelt terroir near Rosport. Luc Roeder began restoring his first vines in 2009 and works dry-stone terraces, with an entirely manual harvest. The estate produces an old-vine Elbling, Pinot, Chardonnay and the Cuvée Kimmfrächen crémant.
