The estate

Maison Viticole Roeder is a small organic estate in Steinheim, on the Hoelt terroir near Rosport. Luc Roeder began restoring his first vines in 2009 and works dry-stone terraces, with an entirely manual harvest. The estate produces an old-vine Elbling, Pinot, Chardonnay and the Cuvée Kimmfrächen crémant.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Sample frequently asked questions — correct the answers, they are yours.

Are your wines organic?

Yes, the estate works organically, and the harvest is done entirely by hand.

Can we visit without an appointment?

To buy, yes, during opening hours. For a guided tasting, better to let us know first.

Do you take groups?

Yes, up to about thirty people. Tell us how many you are and when.

Do you deliver?

Within Luxembourg, yes, from six bottles. Write to us and we will arrange it.
