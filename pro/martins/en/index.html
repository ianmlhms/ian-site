Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Sample frequently asked questions — correct the answers, they are yours.

What time do you open?

5.30 a.m. on weekdays, 7.30 a.m. at the weekend. The bread is warm from opening.

How far ahead for a cake?

Two days is enough for most cakes. For a large one, allow a week.

Can you write a name on the cake?

Yes, just say so with the order.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes — coffee, galão and pastries, to take away or to eat in.
