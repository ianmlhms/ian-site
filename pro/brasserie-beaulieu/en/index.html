The brasserie

Brasserie Beaulieu is a neighbourhood brasserie in Bonnevoie. Our wine list brings together French, Luxembourgish and other growers, alongside a weekday menu, burgers, croque-monsieur and fish and chips. The terrace seats around forty, there is brunch on Sundays and music on Thursdays.
