The place

Café de la Gare is a café and restaurant serving traditional food, right opposite Munsbach station. People come to eat, for a drink on the terrace, or for a game of skittles.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Sample frequently asked questions — correct the answers, they are yours.

Do I need to book?

Not compulsory, but safer at the weekend and for the terrace. A phone call is enough.

Is there a terrace?

Yes, it opens as soon as the weather is good enough.

Can we play skittles?

Yes. The skittle alley is booked in advance, especially in the evening.

Do you take groups and family celebrations?

Yes, the room is laid out for long tables. Write and tell us what you have in mind.

Which languages do you speak?

Luxembourgish, French, German and English.
