KartTracker

KartTracker for iPhone & Apple Watch

Help and support

A free karting lap timer. The Apple Watch times your laps with GPS while you drive; the iPhone app shows your sessions with lap charts, a track map and personal bests. Made by Ian Mulheims in Luxembourg as a hobby project. No ads, no subscription.

In short

  • Automatic lap timing at the start/finish line.
  • Last laps, best lap and heart rate live on your wrist.
  • Saved tracks with personal bests.
  • Lap chart, map, CSV export and web links on the iPhone.
  • Works without an account.
  • A demo mode shows everything without a kart.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  • An Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. The laps are timed on the watch.
  • An outdoor track with GPS reception. Indoor tracks usually have no GPS signal.

Getting started

1 Install

Install KartTracker on the iPhone. The watch app installs with it. If it does not, open the Watch app on the iPhone › Available Apps.

2 Start a session

At the track, open KartTracker on the watch and tap Start Session. Allow Location and Health when asked.

3 Set the start line

On your first visit, drive to the start/finish line and tap Set Start Line. The track is saved. Next time, pick it from the list and the line is already set.

4 Drive

Every crossing of the line counts a lap. Your wrist gets a tap for each lap and a stronger one for a new best.

5 Stop

Hold the Stop button for one second. The session is saved and appears on the iPhone.

Try it without a kart (demo mode)

On the watch, tap Demo Lap on the home screen. A simulated kart drives laps of about 20 seconds, so you can see live timing.

On the iPhone, tap Add Demo Session on the Sessions tab (or in the ••• menu). Demo sessions are marked DEMO and are never uploaded automatically.

Questions and answers

No laps are counted

Set the start line while driving over it, or just before you start. The line takes its direction from your movement. Cross it in the same direction every lap. Laps shorter than 8 seconds are ignored.

“Getting GPS fix…” does not go away

Wait in the open air for a minute. If Location is off for KartTracker, turn it on in the watch’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

No heart rate

Allow KartTracker to read heart rate and save workouts in the Health permissions (iPhone: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, or the Watch’s Settings › Health). Without it, lap timing still works, but may pause while your wrist is down.

A session is missing on the iPhone

Open KartTracker on both devices and wait a minute. If it is still missing, go to the iPhone app › Settings › Apple Watch › Fetch Sessions from Apple Watch, or on the watch go to History › Send All to iPhone.

Rename or delete a track

In the iPhone app, go to Tracks and touch and hold a track.

Share a session

Open the session on the iPhone › share button › Share Web Link. Stop Sharing removes the web page again. In Settings › Sharing you can leave heart rate out.

What is the karting account?

It is optional. Link the app in Settings › Karting account to save sessions to a free ian.lu account and see them in a browser on My karting. The app works fully without it.

Delete my karting account

In the iPhone app, go to Settings › Karting account › Delete karting account…, or do it directly on My karting. This deletes the account and all sessions saved in it. Sessions on your devices stay until you delete them there.

Delete everything

Delete sessions by swiping in the app. Stop sharing shared sessions. Delete the karting account if you made one. Then delete the app.

Privacy

Your sessions, location and heart rate stay on your devices unless you share a session or link a karting account. Read the full privacy policy.

Contact and support

Email konto@ian.lu. I usually answer within a few days.

Safety

Use the watch only when it is safe, follow the rules of your track, and never operate the app while driving. It times laps by itself.
